# Controls Exchange brand system

## Core idea
Controls Exchange is a practical trade network for keeping building controls in service. The brand should feel trustworthy, technical, fast and circular rather than flashy or consumer-like.

## Name
**Controls Exchange**

Use the full name in public-facing copy. `ControlsX` is reserved for compact technical contexts such as the web-app short name.

## Primary tagline
**Find the part. Keep the building running.**

Use this as the main public line across the homepage, social cards and launch material.

## Supporting positioning
**The trade network for obsolete, surplus and hard-to-source BMS controls.**

Good supporting themes:
- Extend the life of installed building controls.
- Turn surplus and dead stock into useful inventory.
- Help engineers source urgent replacement parts faster.
- Connect verified trade buyers and suppliers.

## Colour palette
- **CX Green** `#0A7A4A` — primary brand colour; reliability, trade, circularity.
- **Mint** `#4ADE80` — accent; action and forward motion.
- **Charcoal** `#1F2D32` — primary text/dark surface.
- **Soft Neutral** `#F4F6F7` — page and brand-board background.
- **Deep Green** `#064E3B` — dark gradient/support colour.
- **Soft Mint** `#EAF7F0` — light accent surface.

Avoid introducing unrelated blues/purples as primary UI colours.

## Typography
Primary family: **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

Use system sans-serif fallbacks if the hosted font is unavailable. Keep headings compact and confident; body copy should remain plain and readable.

## Logo files
- `static/brand/mark.svg` — icon/favicons/compact UI.
- `static/brand/logo-nav.svg` — light-background navigation wordmark.
- `static/brand/logo-light.svg` — dark-background/footer lockup with tagline.
- `static/brand/social-card.svg` — social/share preview artwork.

Do not stretch, recolour or add effects to the logo outside these approved variants. Leave at least one quarter of the mark width as clear space around it.

## Voice
Write like a knowledgeable trade platform, not an advertising agency.

Prefer:
- direct statements;
- part numbers, stock, condition and response times;
- clear explanations of what happens next;
- short, credible calls to action.

Avoid:
- hype such as “revolutionary”, “world-leading” or “guaranteed”;
- implying Controls Exchange owns, certifies or warranties supplier stock unless that becomes true;
- invented supplier counts, transaction volumes, savings, testimonials or success rates;
- calling quote-derived price data “completed transaction prices”.

## Commercial wording
Buyers are free. Founding suppliers receive the configured founding trial only after company verification. Do not imply that users need a payment card before the trial begins.

## Sustainability wording
It is reasonable to explain that reusing serviceable legacy stock can extend equipment life and reduce unnecessary replacement. Avoid quantified carbon/waste claims until there is a defensible methodology and real data.

## Brand/legal checks before broad launch
The product name should receive a proper UK trademark/domain/company-name clearance before significant marketing spend. A basic web search is not a substitute for an IP professional or an official UKIPO search.
