Privacy notice

Controls Exchange stores account details, company information, inventory uploaded by suppliers and RFQ data required to operate the platform.

Information collected

Account name, work email, company name and location; supplier inventory data; buyer RFQs; supplier responses; order/PO and fulfilment metadata; order messages and documents uploaded by participants; API/webhook configuration and usage; and basic server/session information needed for authentication and security.

Purpose

Data is used to operate search, supplier verification, RFQ routing, buyer/supplier order workflow, account administration, API/webhook integrations, subscription entitlements, marketplace analytics and transactional notifications.

Billing provider

If paid billing is enabled, Stripe processes payment details on its hosted Checkout/Billing Portal. Controls Exchange stores Stripe customer/subscription identifiers and billing status needed to manage access; card numbers are not stored by this application.

Inventory visibility

Supplier inventory is not included in public search until the supplier company is verified by a platform administrator.

Production note

This is an MVP privacy notice, not final legal advice. Before public launch, add your legal entity, controller address, lawful bases, retention schedule, processor list, cookie details and data-subject contact process.
